Brand Identity & Packaging Design

A soft and elegant visual identity created for a fragrance and personal-care brand, combining feminine details, sophisticated typography and playful strawberry-inspired visuals.

The Concept

Soft.
Feminine.
Elegant.

Scents by Yara uses a delicate visual direction that combines luxury-inspired typography with a playful strawberry motif.

The overall identity is intentionally clean, allowing the typography and packaging details to become the main focus.

Pink creates a soft and youthful atmosphere, while subtle gold lines add a refined and premium feeling.
